SEO typically takes 3 to 6 months to show significant results. The exact timeframe depends on:

  • Keyword competition level
  • Current website authority
  • Content quality and quantity
  • Technical SEO health

Quick wins can appear in 30-60 days, but sustainable growth requires 6-12 months.

White-hat SEO uses ethical techniques that follow search engine guidelines. It includes:

  • Quality content creation
  • Natural link building
  • Technical optimization
  • User experience improvements

White-hat SEO ensures long-term sustainable rankings without the risk of Google penalties.

Yes, Local SEO is crucial for businesses targeting customers in specific areas of Bangladesh. Local SEO helps you appear in Google Maps, local search results, and "near me" queries. It increases calls, foot traffic, and inquiries from nearby customers.

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) are modern SEO techniques:

  • AEO: Helps your content appear in Google's Featured Snippets and voice search
  • GEO: Helps your content appear in AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity

Both are essential for 2025+ SEO.

LLM SEO (Large Language Model SEO) optimizes your content for AI language models like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. It involves using simple language, structured data, factual information, and entity-based content so AI tools can easily understand and cite your content.
